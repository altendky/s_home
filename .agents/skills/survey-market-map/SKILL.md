# Survey Market Map

Build a defensible map of a market without turning it into a ranking exercise.
This skill may be used alone or as the discovery component of `survey`.

Do not contact vendors, create accounts, purchase anything, or perform other
external mutations unless separately requested.

## Establish The Boundary

Start from the requested function, relevant application, seed solutions,
constraints, eligibility rule, and exclusion rule. If used within a full
survey, accept those fields from the coordinator. Otherwise infer them from
the request and ask only when an ambiguity would materially change the market
being mapped.

## Map Mechanisms Before Brands

Identify materially different ways to perform the function. Separate families
when mechanism, role, access, workflow, required complements, serviceability,
safety, or ownership cost changes. Keep adjacent alternatives visible without
misclassifying them as equivalents.

## Discover Manufacturers And Products Iteratively

Use manufacturer findings to discover products and product findings to reveal
additional manufacturers, families, competitors, and possible source designs.
Check relevant market channels, which may include:

- general-purpose and industry-specific manufacturers;
- category specialists and professional or industrial suppliers;
- premium and value-oriented suppliers;
- OEM, aftermarket, distributor, and private-label channels; and
- direct, specialist-retail, and conventional distribution.

Also inspect relevant publicly accessible practitioner and owner communities,
such as Reddit, specialist forums, Stack Exchange sites, public mailing lists,
and independent user groups, when they may reveal less-visible products,
regional options, useful terminology, application-specific experience, or
recurring concerns. Treat these as discovery and experiential sources, not
automatically authoritative evidence, and do not bypass access controls.

For globally traded physical products, normally check North America, Europe,
Japan, and any additional region important to the category. Explain why a
region is irrelevant when its omission could otherwise imply a coverage gap.

For each relevant manufacturer, determine only what the map needs: category
focus, product families, market position, support and distribution model,
product-specific origin when documented, and whether it designs, manufactures,
or appears to relabel the product.

## Handle Relationship Claims Carefully

Prefer primary product pages, catalogs, manuals, drawings, and support terms,
then authorized distributors, technical reviews, and detailed owner reports.
Treat snippets and marketplace listings as discovery leads.

Classify consequential claims as **Documented**, **Corroborated**, **Inferred**,
or **Unknown**. Do not infer product origin from company headquarters. Similar
appearance, specifications, packaging, or wording can justify investigating a
relationship but does not prove shared design, manufacture, ownership, or
private labeling.

## Maintain Coverage

Keep a ledger of mechanisms, product families, manufacturers, channels, and
regions checked. For each materially distinct eligible family, return:

- the function and mechanism;
- representative manufacturers and products;
- market and distribution position;
- relationship or origin claims with evidence state;
- adjacent alternatives and exclusions; and
- coverage gaps or unresolved relationships.

Stop when the meaningful families are represented, relevant regions and
channels have been checked, additional searches no longer change the map, and
remaining gaps are explicit. Do not recommend a universal winner unless the
user separately asks for comparison or selection.
