# Survey Application Fit

Determine what the application requires, whether the candidate satisfies those
requirements, how strong the evidence is, and what would resolve important
uncertainty. This skill may be used alone or within `survey`.

Never imply that a measurement, vendor contact, purchase, installation, or
physical test occurred unless the user confirms it.

## Define The Interface

Identify the application-specific facts that control compatibility, such as:

- exact device, model, part, revision, or workflow;
- mechanical dimensions, geometry, access, direction of force, and capacity;
- electrical, data, protocol, software, consumable, or ecosystem interfaces;
- environmental conditions, duty cycle, accuracy, safety, and failure
  consequences; and
- required complementary products, adapters, modifications, or procedures.

Use applicable local records, manuals, drawings, parts references, standards,
and service information before relying on analogy. A category specialist may
identify which dimensions or properties matter; this skill judges whether the
available evidence establishes the match.

## Evaluate Compatibility Evidence

Prefer exact-application documentation or testing. Classify the result as:

1. explicitly listed or tested in the exact application;
2. dimensionally or technically matched;
3. strongly suggested by an equivalent application;
4. plausible but unverified; or
5. incompatible or outside the required function.

Classify consequential supporting claims as **Documented**, **Corroborated**,
**Inferred**, or **Unknown**. Separate a product's general quality from its fit
for this application.

Do not derive false precision from photographs. State what an image supports
visually and what still requires a dimension, drawing, manual, or physical
test. Treat nominal sizes and marketing compatibility claims cautiously when
tolerances, revisions, access, adapters, or regional variants could matter.

## Resolve Blocking Unknowns

For each unknown that could change the conclusion, recommend the least costly
reliable next step, such as:

- locate a missing manual, drawing, compatibility table, or origin statement;
- measure the existing interface or spare part;
- compare a standard, part number, protocol, or known equivalent;
- find an exact-application report;
- ask the manufacturer one narrowly framed technical question; or
- use a return-friendly source for a controlled fit or performance test.

When compatibility depends on a safety-critical or vehicle-specific repair
procedure, also use the applicable authoritative service-information skill or
source. This skill establishes fit; it does not replace the repair procedure.

## Report Proportionally

For a narrow question, lead with the compatibility conclusion and confidence,
then give the controlling requirements, supporting evidence, blockers, and
next validation step. Within a full survey, return the same structured findings
to the coordinator. Do not introduce unrelated alternatives or launch broad
market discovery unless the user asks.
